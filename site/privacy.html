Ħżuż tal-Bini – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Ħżuż tal-Bini ("the app") is a calculator for setting out stone arches and stairs. It is published by Matthew, Malta. This policy explains what data the app uses.

Data you enter

The sizes and settings you type into the app stay on your phone. They are saved only on your device so the app remembers them next time. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Files you create

PDF and DXF files are created on your phone. They are only shared when you choose to send or save them using your phone's share menu.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. Google tells the app which purchases your Google account owns, so the app can unlock them.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information, such as advertising ID, approximate location from IP address, and how ads are viewed, to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. Paid versions show no ads. See Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Bug reports

If you use "Irraporta problema", the report (your description, the sizes you entered and your phone model) is only sent if you choose to send it by WhatsApp or email.

Children

The app is meant for adults working in construction and is not aimed at children.
